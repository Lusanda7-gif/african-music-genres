How to read the labels

Type: modern is a named contemporary or popular style. Traditional is a traditional style or performance practice.

Origin status: strong means the genre is well known as associated with this country. Shared means the style is shared across borders and doesn't claim exclusive origin. Debated means scholars disagree about where it began.

Amapiano

Modern Strong

Description

Amapiano is a South African electronic dance music genre characterised by piano melodies, deep basslines and repetitive rhythms.

Origin and originality

Amapiano emerged in South African townships around 2012, but its exact place of origin remains debated.

Classification

South African-developed modern electronic dance music genre.
