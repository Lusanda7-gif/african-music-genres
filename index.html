How to read the labels

Type: modern is a named contemporary or popular style. Traditional is a traditional style or performance practice.

Origin status: strong means the genre is well known as associated with this country. Shared means the style is shared across borders and doesn't claim exclusive origin. Debated means scholars disagree about where it began.
